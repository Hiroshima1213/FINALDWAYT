DWAYT / FAQ

QUESTIONS?

Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. Express delivery is available during checkout.
DWAYT accepts GCash, Maya, and Cash on Delivery. GCash and Maya payments are verified by our team before shipping.
Tops, bottoms and dresses come in S, M, L and XL. You choose your size when you add an item to your bag, and it appears on your order. Between sizes? Go up for a relaxed fit, or message us and we will help you choose.
If your item arrives damaged or in the wrong size, message us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos. Items must be unworn with tags attached. Exchanges depend on available stock.
Please contact DWAYT as soon as possible after placing your order. Cancellation requests are subject to order status.
